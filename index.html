Request an order

Ready to place an order? Fill out the form below and tell Jean what you’re looking for. Please include as much detail as possible, including the type of item, quantity, flavors, colors, theme, dietary needs, and any other preferences that will help Jean understand your request. Jean will follow up to confirm availability, pricing, and the final details.

  • Recommended notice: 3–7 days for standard orders.
  • Large or custom orders may require additional notice.
  • Final pricing depends on size, design, quantity, pickup, local delivery in Bend, Oregon, or shipping needs for eligible desserts.
